A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT WANT TO KNOW
Can I combine different media formats?+
Yes. Share your audience and markets so the team can discuss a mix of outdoor LED, indoor digital screens and cinema. Availability and delivery terms are confirmed in the proposal.
How do I know which locations are right for my brand?+
Start with your objective, audience, desired cities and campaign period. EYESARK can discuss relevant locations and formats, then confirm the exact inventory before you book.
What creative do I need to provide?+
Requirements vary by screen orientation, format and placement. Ask for the technical specifications before producing your creative, including dimensions, duration and file requirements.
Can agencies enquire on behalf of their clients?+
Yes. Choose “Agency / media partnership” in the enquiry form and include your client’s markets, timing and preferred formats.
